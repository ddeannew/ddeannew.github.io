I recently published an article with The Dorsal Column, a neuroscience communication journal started up at Western University. I'm very proud of this piece!

The article is titled [When Math Meets Dr. Seuss: Our Brain’s Shared System for Mathematics and Language](https://songsuwo.ca/thedorsalcolumn/vol7-iss1-deanne-wah).

Did you know that math and language are linked? How deeply ingrained are these two systems? This article explores how the brains of children and adults process math and language, and how similar these two systems are!
